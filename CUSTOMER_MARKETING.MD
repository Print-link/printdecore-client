- **Launch gating:** Posts 1–12 run while Printdecore is pre-launch (waitlist CTA). Posts 13–24 assume soft-launch with app available (download CTA).
- **Visual identity:** Every post should feature the Printdecore yellow accent (`#fbbf24`) — in a highlighted word, a pin on the map, a checkout button, a corner logo, or the "I'm happy" escrow button.
- **Localization:** Swap city names and landmarks when reposting for other markets (Lagos, Nairobi, Johannesburg). The story beats are country-agnostic.
