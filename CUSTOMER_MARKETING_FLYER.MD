# Printdecore — Customer Flyer Pack

**Formats in scope:** A4, A5, Instagram square (1080×1080), Instagram vertical (1080×1350), 9:16 story, WhatsApp status (1080×1920).
**Universal palette:** Printdecore Yellow `#fbbf24` · Deep Black `#111111` · Off-White `#FAFAFA` · Accent Grey `#6B7280` · Success Green `#10B981`.
**Universal typefaces:** Display — heavy/black sans (Inter Black, Gilroy Heavy, or Satoshi Black). Body — Inter Regular/Medium.
**Universal footer on every flyer:**
> `printdecore.com` · `@printdecore` · Ghana · Download on iOS & Android

Each flyer below is a **self-contained static asset**. Hand the section directly to a designer — it has the headline, subhead, body, visual direction, CTA, and layout notes.

## Design System Notes for the Studio

- **Grid:** 8-column on A-size formats; 4-column on Instagram ratios. Always 32px outer margin, 24px inner gutter.
- **Corner radius:** 16px on cards; 24px on CTA buttons; 9999px on badges.
- **Shadow:** Flyers are print-safe — use subtle shadow only on digital exports; strip for print runs.
- **Logo placement:** Top-left on A-size flyers. Bottom-center on IG vertical. Never larger than 40px tall.
- **CTA button:** Yellow fill, black text, black 1px stroke. Always left-aligned arrow glyph (`→`).
- **QR code rules:** Min 180×180px. Always with a short URL label below (`printdecore.com/app`).
- **Accessibility:** Body text no smaller than 14pt on A5, 16pt on A4. Contrast ratio 4.5:1 minimum.
- **Language variants:** Keep headlines English for pan-West-African use; localize body copy to Twi / Ga / Pidgin for hyper-local prints in Accra, Kumasi, and Tema.
